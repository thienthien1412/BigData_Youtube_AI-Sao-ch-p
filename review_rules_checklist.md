
# CHECKLIST XAC MINH VIDEO NHAC AI

## 1. Kiem tra noi dung
- [ ] Video co chua bai nhac hoac phan trinh dien nhac thuc te.
- [ ] Khong phai video huong dan, quang cao, tin tuc hoac thao luan don thuan ve AI.
- [ ] Khong chi dua vao tieu de, hashtag hoac tu khoa tim kiem.

## 2. Kiem tra bang chung AI
- [ ] Ghi ro bang chung lien quan den viec tao nhac hoac tao giong hat.
- [ ] Luu nguon bang chung va noi dung bang chung.
- [ ] Phan biet AI tao anh/video voi AI tao nhac.
- [ ] Neu khong du bang chung, giu trang thai review.

## 3. Phan loai
- ai_cover: AI tao hoac mo phong giong hat de cover ca khuc.
- ai_original: Bai nhac goc co su tham gia cua AI trong qua trinh tao nhac.
- both: Video dap ung ca hai nhom.
- uncertain: Chua du can cu de phan loai.

## 4. Quyet dinh
- keep: Da kiem tra va co bang chung phu hop.
- review: Can kiem tra them.
- exclude: Khong thuoc pham vi nghien cuu.
- Khong lay binh luan cua video chua duoc duyet keep.

## 5. Bao toan du lieu
- Khong xoa video va binh luan cu chi vi thay doi trang thai.
- Luu ly do va thoi diem thay doi trang thai.
- Khong thu thap binh luan khi chua duoc phe duyet.
